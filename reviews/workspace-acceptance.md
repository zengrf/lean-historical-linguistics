# Researcher acceptance protocol — not yet conducted

Status: **pending independent participants**. Automated checks are in
`reports/workspace/verification.json`. They do not fill in this record.

Recruit at least five historical linguists or philologists, including experience
with Indo-European and Sino-Tibetan materials and at least one researcher who
works directly with manuscripts or multiple editions. Obtain their agreement
before retaining identifiable feedback. Use a shared, source-permitted dataset
and one participant-supplied example; do not publish private research data.

Give each participant the following tasks without exposing JSON or Lean syntax:

1. Import a wordlist with missing, unknown and zero observations. Explain how
   those states differ and locate the source of a particular reading.
2. Add a competing manuscript reading, select it for comparison, then restore
   the previous reading without losing either source.
3. Enter an ordered sound-change history, including a conditioned change and
   deletion. Predict a result before running the reconstruction.
4. Compare two hypotheses with different selected daughter observations and
   word-shape constraints. Explain what “all reconstructions” and the displayed
   whole-wordlist count mean.
5. Inspect a candidate's derivation and identify a conflicting cognate set.
   Recognize an exhausted search as incomplete.
6. Save, recover a browser draft, handle a competing-window save, restore an old
   revision, and restore a workspace backup into a separate directory.
7. Export to a tool used in the participant's own practice. Verify the printed
   reading, segmentation, source locator, cognacy and missing-data conventions.

Record start/end times, successful completion, assistance, wrong inferences,
lost or unexpectedly transformed data, and terminology the participant had to
ask about. Capture the exact software commit and input hashes. Do not interpret
task speed as evidence of linguistic correctness.

Acceptance: every participant can complete tasks 1–6 after at most one short
orientation; at least 90% of those task attempts succeed without intervention;
no unresolved loss of data, unannounced change of hypothesis scope, or mistaken
completeness claim remains. Document failures and repeat affected tasks after
fixes. Separately test keyboard-only operation, screen-reader names and focus,
zoom at 200%, contrast in all three appearances, and the smallest supported
viewport. These are proposed release criteria, not measured results.
