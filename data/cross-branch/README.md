# Cross-branch comparisons

`dossiers.json` contains 30 comparisons drawn from the repository's primary papers: eight concerning *sr*, five Tibetan–Burmese rhyme comparisons, five Tibetan morphophonological comparisons, six Old Chinese prefix discussions and six suffix comparisons. Two dossiers explicitly compare morphological functions rather than cognate lexical roots. One proposed etymology is rejected in the primary source; the remaining dossiers retain unresolved historical questions.

Every dossier records source pages, written or transcribed forms, reconstruction status, morphology, competing analyses, topology assumptions and evidence that could distinguish the accounts. Chinese graphs, Middle Chinese scholarly transcriptions and Old Chinese reconstructions occupy separate fields. A missing transcription is left absent. Original rhyme texts and phonetic series have not been independently collated.

The manually entered Middle Chinese display strings use `ṣ` for the retroflex sibilant printed as IPA `ʂ` in Jacques's paper. This explicit display convention does not introduce a phonetic distinction. The executable projections list their tokens separately; independent transcription review remains required.

`source-scopes.json` distinguishes VanBik's PKC, PCC, PNC and Proto-Maraic from Button's broader PNC. It also keeps the hypothetical PTB node separate from PST and records two conditional topology assumptions. Button's tone categories are not converted to VanBik's labels or to universal pitch values.

These files were committed in `2ed6130` before the experiment. Each primary PDF is identified by SHA-256 and examined pages. Some competing theories are known through the cited author's discussion; that secondary attribution is explicit. The research copies remain in the local library where redistribution permission is unestablished.

The executable projections and documentary limits are described in [M6 delivery](../../docs/12-m6-delivery.md). The [review packet](../../reviews/sino-tibetan-packet.json) includes all 30 dossiers.
