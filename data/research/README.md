# Research examples

These finite inputs accompany the [workbench guide](../../docs/14-research-workbench.md).
They are generated by `python scripts/build_research_examples.py` and reproduced
with `--check`. The frozen M5/M6 experiments are separate.

| File | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `tone-paradigm.json` | VanBik smooth nominal tone correspondences; two explicit readings of the printed Hakha Lai tone-2 discrepancy |
| `stem-paradigm.json` | VanBik ATTACH [2], with a stipulated linked Form I/II alternation and Hakha Lai invariant stem; tones omitted |
| `affix-control.json` | Synthetic affixation, vowel alternation and stem-conditioned tone example |
| `evidence-control.json` | Synthetic M4 specification with two minimal inconsistent observation subsets |
| `germanic-chronology.json` | Orders of the existing M5 Grimm, Verner and initial-stress fragments on accent-marked VCV controls |
| `source-readings.json` | Source PDF hash, examined pages, rendered-table checks, discrepancy and interpretation limits |

Source-worked examples formalize the stated projections only. They neither
certify the source's historical analyses nor provide specialist review. See
[source attribution](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md); the new code and synthetic
controls do not relicense the cited books.
